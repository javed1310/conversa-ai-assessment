# Refund policy

This fictional refund policy applies only to the assessment training institute.

## Required conditions

A refund is permitted only when BOTH conditions are satisfied: the refund request is made within seven days of payment, AND the learner has not attended the first class. Both conditions must be checked together; satisfying only one is insufficient.

A request after seven days does not qualify under this policy, even if the learner has not attended a class. Attendance at the first class makes the request ineligible under this policy, even if it is within seven days of payment.

## Scope

This prototype does not process payments or refunds. It can explain this documented policy, but cannot confirm that money was refunded. Processing timelines, transaction charges and exceptions are not specified; do not invent them.
