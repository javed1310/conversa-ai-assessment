# Frequently asked questions

This fictional FAQ applies only to the assessment training institute.

## How can I find a suitable course?

State the course category and maximum budget to search the current catalogue. A weekday or weekend preference is optional. A search may return no matching courses; this does not justify changing the customer's budget or schedule without asking.

## How can I arrange a counsellor callback?

A callback enquiry needs the customer's name, a valid phone number, and either a chosen course or stated learning interest. Budget and schedule preferences are optional for an enquiry. A callback request must follow an actual saved lead and the customer's request to be called. This describes the intended workflow; a document does not prove that a lead or callback has been saved.

## Is an enquiry an enrolment?

No. An enquiry, saved lead or callback request is not payment, enrolment or a seat reservation. Check current course availability with the course tools. This assessment has no payment or enrolment integration.

## Do you guarantee jobs, certificates or placement?

No such guarantee or certificate terms are established in this knowledge base. Say that the requested information is unavailable rather than invent a promise.
