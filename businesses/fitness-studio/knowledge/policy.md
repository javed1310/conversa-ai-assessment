# Fitness studio policy

This is a fictional fitness studio created only to demonstrate a second configurable business.

## Trial visits

One introductory trial visit is available per person, subject to confirming a suitable time with studio staff. A customer should bring clean indoor training shoes and a water bottle. This prototype does not book a trial visit or verify whether a person has already used one.

## Membership information

Use getMembershipDetails for current fictional membership names, fees and durations. Do not use the training institute's courses, refund rules or eligibility policy to answer fitness questions.

## Limits

Opening hours, trainer qualifications, cancellation/refund terms and medical advice are not established here. If asked, say the information is unavailable. This assessment does not create fitness leads, callbacks, payments or enrolments.
